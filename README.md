# bmo-voc

The customers' voice box: bug reports and suggestions a customer sends to the maker, and the service the maker reads
them in. A person signed in to the console who is not a platform administrator sends a report, with screenshots and
files, and reads their own reports with the maker's answers; the platform administrators read every report of every
company, move it through its states and answer it, each change kept with who and when. The unit is the master of its
own data, kept in its own SQLite store and attachment folder. `docs/design.md` states what it is and `docs/api.md` its
boundary: every route, the events it publishes, the calls it makes and its settings. Every other unit is reached
through its API.

## Tests

`cargo test` (through the testbed) runs the crate's one harness, `tests/main.rs`, over a stand-in of the account
service on a loopback port, which records the calls it takes, and a SQLite file and attachment folder in a scratch
folder: reports sent whole with their files or refused whole, read by their sender alone and by the maker across
companies, moved through their states with their history, answered from both sides, counted unread for each side,
their attachments handed inline or as downloads, the events each reader is given, the console face's guard and its
served manifest read through bmo-ui's own reading on the release the client vendors, the OpenAPI document, and the
repository's own checks.

`client/` holds its own tests, run by `bun test` there: the four word packs (English, Korean, Japanese and Simplified
Chinese) agreeing key for key and placeholder for placeholder, every key a screen writes and every error code of
`docs/api.md` standing in each, and no Korean in the sources; the API module's calls over a stand-in of the service.
`client/tests/browser/` drives both faces of the screen in a real browser, at a desktop's width and a phone's, over
the bundle's dev host and a stand-in of the service.

## Layout

| Folder | Holds |
|---|---|
| `src/` | The service and its command: the routes, who calls and which side they are on, the store of reports, attachments, states, replies and read marks with its migrations, the calls to the account service, the event stream, the console face's declaration and the OpenAPI document |
| `client/` | The console bundle: its Nuxt project, the vendored bmo-ui release and the layer's patch of the component library its install applies, the build that writes the module, stylesheet, words and layer the manifest names, its pages and components, its words, its own tests, and the dev host that stands for a console in a browser check |
| `deploy/` | The service's user unit |
| `docs/` | The canonical statements of what the service is and its boundary, and the OpenAPI document |
| `tests/` | The crate's one test harness and the stand-in of its peer it runs over |
| `.graph/` | The code graph's settings; its area files are generated by `bmo graph` and untracked |

At the root stand the Cargo manifest, `Cargo.lock`, `testbed.toml`, `install.toml` (what installing the unit on a fresh machine needs), `rustfmt.toml`, `settings.toml` (the defaults
built into the binary), `CLAUDE.md`, `PERSONAL-DATA.md` and `LICENSE`.
